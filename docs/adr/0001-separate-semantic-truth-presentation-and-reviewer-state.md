# Separate semantic truth, presentation, and reviewer state

The system stores an immutable semantic artifact for one Source Snapshot. Presentation and reviewer-specific state remain separate projections. Semantic truth is the domain name for the recorded assertions and epistemic states; it is not a claim that model-assessed semantics cannot be wrong.

Trusted acquisition establishes Code Comparison Identity without verification-specific subjects. Each Verification Evidence Identity carries its own actual execution commit or tree, provider provenance, scope, consumed payload hashes, and eligibility under the pinned comparison inputs. Source Basis Identity includes consumed metadata, the complete in-scope verification set and eligibility states, supplied trace, and source and freshness policies. Source Snapshot also binds the complete mechanical Git-tree inventory, Semantic Input Manifest, and analysis policies. New context expands the manifest and requires new snapshot-bound analysis.

Snapshots are pinned content and timed observation bundles. Acquisition detects resource membership and version drift, tries at most three times, and records unknown consistency when required observations or stable capture are unavailable. Observational stability does not promise an atomic provider state. At presentation and decision recording, the controller reconciles the full Source Basis Identity and result expiry. Observations have a finite freshness deadline. Local compare-and-swap protects the exact SourceSnapshotId and PublishedViewIdentity when recording reviewer state; it is not a lock over GitHub resources or live merge authorization.

Models cannot alter policy or privileged state. Generator and validator outputs remain untrusted. The controller deterministically enforces assertion and evidence bindings, scope witnesses, schemas, and mechanical check obligations. Other support remains visibly model assessed and fallible. Disagreement, ambiguous support, or failed obligations becomes inference, uncertainty, or a gap. Coverage retains residual state and an independent closure-falsification pass; exhaustive mechanical change inventory does not establish complete semantic understanding.

Every renderer treats source content as inert text or data. Trusted templates and semantic operators preserve labels, relationships, support basis, and uncertainty. Controller-owned URL and resource policy prevents source-directed executable content or network requests. Validate output structure before hashing. PublishedViewIdentity binds semantic artifact identity, presentation and renderer versions, and concrete content hashes. Reviewer notes and decisions retain the exact view, snapshot, and observed freshness.

The [Solo evaluation protocol](../../work/critical-only/evaluation-protocol.md) binds each scored trial to a captured source bundle and freezes instrument, treatments, assignments, decision rule, and failure policy. Product failures retain assignment through a frozen baseline fallback. Missing outcomes remain in primary and sensitivity analyses. The protocol measures exploratory creator-only performance on its evidence-adjudicated instrument.

The MVP recomputes semantics whenever snapshot identity changes and canonicalizes publication atomically under the complete snapshot and policy key. These boundaries preserve reproducibility and state attribution while exposing observation limits and residual model error.
