How these four were chosen

Three builders each made a warm and a dark direction. A separate judge scored all six on polish, a 30-second read, code readability, color, fit to the Control layout, and an 800 px width. Warm paper and Dark product come from the same builder and scored 27 and 28 of 30.

Rejected: builder 2 warm and builder 2 dark leave the Doc and the code blank until you scroll them into view. Builder 3 warm and builder 3 dark are clean but look almost the same, and their videos overlap titles during transitions.
